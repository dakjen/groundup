## 3. Office Hours

**What it is.** Live group time with Dr. Merritt, one to two hours, on Zoom. Bring your questions and hear everyone else's. Times are dropped in batches a few times a year, first come, first served, and members RSVP on the Office Hours page. Dr. Merritt pulls up each member's profile before the session.

**Who gets it.**
- Premium: 2 sessions a year.
- Owner: 6 sessions a year.
- Opens after 4 months of continuous membership for Premium, 2 months for Owner. Cancelling and rejoining restarts the clock.
- Founding members: group office hours for their first 5 years.
- Member and Builder: not included (upgrade path).
